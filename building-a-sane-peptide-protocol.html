Building a Sane Peptide Protocol

This is the protocol I’ve chosen for myself—one that has supported my weight management, energy, focus, and overall performance across work, training, and daily life.

Tirzepatide
A cornerstone of my weight-management approach, supporting metabolic efficiency and long-term consistency rather than quick fixes.

Ipamorelin / Tesamorelin
Peptides associated with growth hormone signaling, which I’ve found supportive for hormonal balance, muscle preservation, and recovery as I age and stay active.

GLOW (GHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500)
My beauty and resilience stack. GHK-Cu is widely known for its role in skin quality, elasticity, and overall glow, while BPC-157 and TB-500 are often used to support joint comfort, mobility, and tissue repair.

NAD+
A foundational element for mental clarity and cognitive support—especially helpful when managing long days, decision fatigue, and sustained focus.

Together, this protocol has been a game-changer for me—helping me show up sharper in the office, stronger in the gym, and more present in life. It’s not about doing more; it’s about supporting the body so performance feels sustainable, not forced.
